Core Engineering

Key Features & Technical Innovations

Engineered from scratch to combine nostalgic arcade thrill with modern web standards and authentic STI pride.

Procedural Web Audio Engine

Zero external MP3/WAV dependencies. Generates authentic retro chiptune sine, triangle, and square waveforms dynamically using the native Web Audio API with low latency and dynamic audio compression.

Laoag Landmarks Parallax

Pays tribute to Ilocos Norte heritage with a procedural multi-layer backdrop featuring the historic Laoag Sinking Bell Tower, distant Paoay sand dunes, and the iconic Bangui wind turbines.

Latin Honors Grading System

Translates game proficiency directly into academic tiers: Freshman Flyer (0-4 pts), STI Scholar (5-14 pts), Cum Laude (15-24 pts), Magna Cum Laude (25-39 pts), and Summa Cum Laude (40+ pts).

Verifiable Certificate Generator

Renders an 800x1040 high-resolution official Student Flyer Certificate on an offscreen HTML5 canvas with cryptographic serial hashes, custom student names, and instant one-tap PNG downloads for My Day.

Fluid Cross-Platform Engine

Built with delta-time framerate smoothing and dynamic screen shake. Automatically scales from compact vertical mobile screens (1224x2720 aspect) to wide desktop browsers with touch, mouse, and keyboard bindings.

Zero-Install Instant Play

No heavy APK downloads or app store hurdles. Instant cold starts under 1.5 seconds with browser LocalStorage memory ensuring personal best scores and custom callsigns remain saved safely.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install an APK or app to play Aim High with STI?

No installation or APK sideloading is required! Aim High with STI runs 100% inside your web browser via HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript. Simply click "PLAY GAME" or open the GitHub Pages URL on any device to enjoy instant 60 FPS gameplay.

How do I unlock Latin Honors and the Summa Cum Laude rank?

Your academic ranking updates dynamically as your flight score increases:
• 0–4 points: Freshman Flyer
• 5–14 points: STI Scholar
• 15–24 points: Cum Laude
• 25–39 points: Magna Cum Laude
• 40+ points: Summa Cum Laude 👑

How does the Student Flyer Certificate generator work?

When your flight concludes, the game engine invokes a high-resolution 800x1040 offscreen canvas. It draws the official STI College Laoag insignia, gold foil borders, academic seal, cryptographic verification serial, and your custom name. You can save it as an image with one click to flex on Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok My Day!

Does the game remember my high scores?

Yes! Your all-time personal best and flyer callsign are preserved safely in your browser's LocalStorage memory. Whenever you return to the page, your record and credentials will be waiting for you.

How does sound work without loading any audio files?

The game features a custom procedural sound synthesizer using the native Web Audio API. Every flap, point chime, collision thud, medal flourish, and power-up whoosh is generated purely from math, frequencies, and oscillators in real time with zero network bandwidth!
