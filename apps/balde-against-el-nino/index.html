5 Interactive Survival Stages

The 5 Gameplay Stages

Each level delivers unique mini-game mechanics, satirical dialogue, and civic awareness challenges.

STAGE 1 43°C Heat Index

Catching Rain in the Street

Move your bucket left and right to catch precious raindrops before they evaporate under the heat. Dodge falling debris and mud to keep your collection pure and drinkable!

💧 Target: Collect 15.0 Liters
STAGE 2 Kit Assembly

Assemble the Bucket Filter

Follow the 5-step assembly procedure to install the family water filter kit into your bucket (drill holes, insert food-grade washer, fit ceramic core, tighten nuts, and attach siphon hose).

🔧 5-Step Filtration Assembly Challenge
STAGE 3 Household Dilemma

Household Water Rationing

Ration the filtered water you collected. Make tough survival decisions: allocate drinking water for children, cook daily rice, hydrate the family pet, or take an emergency sponge bath.

⚖️ Strict Water Allocation Budgeting
STAGE 4 Heat Overload

Heat Wave Survival

Fast-paced survival: rapidly pump the aging community water well, irrigate wilting backyard crops, and hydrate family members before heat exhaustion sets in.

🔥 Real-time Well Pumping & Hydration
STAGE 5 (FINAL) Civic Advocacy Ending

Where Is the Permanent Solution?

Select 3 long-term public policies to determine your community ending (Piped Clean Water to Every Kitchen, Forest Watershed Reforestation, and Rainwater Cisterns). Emergency buckets help in disasters, but Filipinos deserve permanent, reliable clean water infrastructure!

Controls & Technology Stack

Game Controls

DESKTOP Use Arrow Keys (Left & Right) or Mouse click to slide the bucket across the screen.
MOBILE Drag with Touch / Swipe Gestures on your phone to catch incoming raindrops.
AUDIO Integrated Sound FX for water splashes, sizzles, and emergency voice instructions.

Technology Stack

Game Engine & Logic Vanilla JavaScript & HTML5 Canvas
UI Styling Tailwind CSS
Audio Integration HTML5 Audio & Web Audio API
Data Storage Client-Side LocalStorage
Deployment Target GitHub Pages (Instant Web Access)
