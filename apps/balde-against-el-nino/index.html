BALDE AGAINST EL NIÑO

"One Bucket, One Family: Can 20 Liters Save Your Household from the Heat Wave?!"

An interactive satirical Pinoy survival web game and civic advocacy project inspired by the real-world El Niño drought crises in the Philippines and official statements advising households to survive with a single bucket (*"balde"*). Experience the authentic daily dilemma: can 20 liters of water truly sustain drinking, cooking rice, emergency sponge baths, and household pets under a blistering 43°C Heat Index?

Stages of Survival

THE 5 GAMEPLAY STAGES

Each level delivers unique mini-game mechanics, comic dialogue, and satirical challenges!

LEVEL 1 43°C HEAT INDEX

Catching Rain in the Street

Where Did the Water Go? — Move your bucket left and right to catch precious raindrops falling from the sky before they evaporate under the blistering sun. Dodge falling debris and mud to keep your water collection pure and drinkable!

💧 Target: Collect 15.0 Liters
LEVEL 2 KIT ASSEMBLY

Assemble the Bucket Filter

Filter Your Own Solution! — Follow the 5-step assembly procedure to install the family water filter kit into your bucket (drill holes, insert food-grade rubber washer, fit ceramic/carbon core, tighten wing nuts, and attach clean siphon hose) before the timer runs out!

🔧 5-Step Filtration Assembly Challenge
LEVEL 3 42°C HOUSEHOLD

Household Water Rationing

One Bucket, One Family! — Ration and budget the actual liters of filtered water you collected in Level 1. Make hard survival decisions: allocate drinking water to protect children from heat stroke, cook daily rice, hydrate the family pet (aspin), or take an emergency sponge bath.

⚖️ Strict Water Allocation Budgeting
LEVEL 4 HEAT OVERLOAD

Heat Wave Survival

43°C Heat Overload! — Fast-paced action survival: rapidly pump the aging community water well, irrigate wilting backyard crops, and hydrate family members before their health meters deplete from heat exhaustion.

🔥 Real-time Well Pumping & Hydration
FINAL LEVEL CIVIC ADVOCACY

Where Is the Permanent Solution?

Long-Term Policy Planning — In the final stage, players select 3 long-term public policies to determine their ending (Piped Clean Water to Every Kitchen, Forest Watershed Reforestation, and Modern Rainwater Cisterns for schools and public halls). The core message: emergency buckets help in disasters, but Filipinos deserve permanent, reliable clean water infrastructure!

CONTROLS & WEB STACK

Game Controls

DESKTOP Use Arrow Keys (Left & Right) or Mouse click to slide the bucket across the screen.
MOBILE Drag with Touch / Swipe Gestures on your phone to catch incoming raindrops.
AUDIO Integrated Sound FX for water splashes, sizzles, and emergency voice instructions.

Technology Stack

Game Engine & Logic Vanilla JavaScript & HTML5 DOM / Canvas
UI Styling Tailwind CSS (Neo-Brutalist Comic Aesthetic)
Audio Integration HTML5 Audio & Web Audio API
Data Storage Client-Side LocalStorage (Leaderboard & High Score)
Deployment Target GitHub Pages (Instant Web Access)
