01 / System Overview

How AgriGuard Protects Fields

Automated 4-stage AI monitoring and deterrence engineered for off-grid farms.

The Agricultural Challenge

Rice and grain crops face persistent damage from birds such as Maya and Kannaway. Manual watching is exhausting, static scarecrows lose effect, and open fields lack reliable electricity.

1. Detect — Edge AI Vision

Smart field hardware (Raspberry Pi 4 + camera) detects incoming birds in real time using YOLO & OpenCV.

2. Alert — Instant Acoustic Scare

Speaker alarm fires immediately to deter birds and prevent crop damage without causing physical harm.

3. Record — Automated Event Log

Flask API stores each detection in SQLite with image snapshot, exact timestamp, and bird stay duration.

4. Measure — Deterrent Scoring

Calculates effectiveness percentage based on how rapidly birds vacate the area after the alarm triggers.
