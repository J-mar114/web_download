How AgriGuard Works

Built to protect crops in real time through monitoring, instant alerting, and field action.

1. Detect

The Agricrow system detects bird activity in the field using smart monitoring hardware.

2. Alert

The alarm speaker is triggered to scare away birds and reduce crop damage immediately.

3. Record

The app logs each event with the time of detection and how long the bird remained in the area.

4. Measure

Effectiveness is calculated based on how quickly the birds leave after the alarm is activated.

Key Features of AgriGuard

Combining Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the Internet of Things (IoT) to deliver modern solutions to the agricultural sector.

Detects bird activity in real time and sends immediate alerts when intruders enter the monitored farm area.

Farm Protection Status

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does AgriGuard detect birds in the farm?

AgriGuard uses its monitoring system to detect bird activity in real time and sends an alert when birds enter the monitored farm area.

What information can I see in the Farm Protection Status?

The app shows the current monitoring condition, alarm activity, and recent bird alerts so you can quickly check the protection status of the farm.

Can I track bird activity and alarm effectiveness?

Yes. AgriGuard records the time of each bird detection, how long the bird remains in the area, and how quickly it responds to the alarm to measure deterrent effectiveness.

What happens when a bird is detected?

The alarm is triggered to help scare the bird away, while the app records the event and updates the farm protection status.
