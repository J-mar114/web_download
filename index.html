Project Exhibition Guidelines

CITS Week 2026 • STI College Laoag

BSIT and BSCS students exhibiting projects for CITS Week can submit their live systems, web games, or capstone deliverables by following these steps:

1
Create Your Project Directory: Add a dedicated folder inside apps/<your-project>/ containing your HTML, assets, or mobile APK.
2
Register in Catalog: Add your project metadata entry inside store-assets/store.js under APPS_CATALOG.
3
CITS Review & Approval: Coordinate with your Capstone Adviser or CITS Officers for exhibition verification.
